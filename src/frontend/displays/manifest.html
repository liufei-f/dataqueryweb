Manifest rows

Candidate rows in the format of LocusCompare's qtl_manifest.tsv, built from the download locations of saved records: the files were listed and their headers read, fixed rules mapped the columns, and the AI read the paper for context, sample size, ancestry and genome build. Every value shows where it came from; a quote counts only when it was found in the text the AI was given (✓), otherwise it is marked ✗ unverified. Open a row to check the evidence, correct a field, then Approve or Reject. Rules {{ rules_version }} are written out in MANIFEST_RULES.md.
